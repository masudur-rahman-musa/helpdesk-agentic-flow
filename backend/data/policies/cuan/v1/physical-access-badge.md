# Physical Access Policy

Document id: CU-POL-008
Version: 1.0
Owner: Head of Information Security
Effective date: 2025-02-03
Review cycle: annual

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs physical access to the premises of Cuan Financial Services Ltd: who may enter, which areas they may enter, and how entry is controlled. It applies to employees, contractors, visitors, and delivery and maintenance personnel. Physical access is managed by facilities together with IT; it is not requested through the system access paths in the Access Control Policy (CU-POL-001), though the same principle applies: entry rights match duties, are individually attributable, and end when the need ends.

Physical and logical security depend on each other. A person who can walk to an unlocked workstation on the operations floor, or into the server room, can bypass much of what our system controls achieve. Our regulator examines premises controls in a payments firm alongside system controls.

## 2. Access cards

Every employee and contractor receives a personal access card from facilities on their first day, issued against the HR System record created under the Joiner, Mover, Leaver Procedure (CU-POL-003). The card carries a photo, is worn visibly inside the premises, and is used by its holder only. Lending a card, holding a door with your card for someone else's entry, or using a colleague's card are each treated as credential sharing under the Acceptable Use Policy (CU-POL-006).

Cards are configured for the zones the holder's duties require, as defined in section 3. Zone changes are requested to facilities by the holder's department manager with a stated reason. Facilities keeps the card register: holder, zones, issue date, and end date. Contractor cards carry the contract end date as an expiry, in line with the Third-party and Contractor Access Policy (CU-POL-009).

## 3. Zones

The premises are divided into three zones.

General offices cover desks, meeting rooms, and common areas. All staff cards open the general offices during building hours. Outside building hours, entry is logged per person and reviewed weekly by facilities.

The operations floor is the area where Customer Support and Operations staff handle customer accounts and payments. Entry is limited to members of those departments, their managers, and named support staff from IT and facilities. Screens on the operations floor face away from walkways, and phones and cameras are not used to capture screens or documents there. Visitors enter the operations floor only under section 4 and only with the department head's approval.

The server and comms room holds network equipment, local servers, and telecoms terminations. Entry is limited to named IT staff and named facilities staff, listed by the head of IT and reviewed quarterly. Every entry is individually logged by the card system. All other persons, including senior management, enter only when escorted by a listed person, and the escort logs the visit. Work by external engineers in this room follows the escort rule for its full duration; an engineer is never left alone in the room.

## 4. Visitors

All visitors sign in at reception, state their host, and receive a numbered visitor badge that visibly differs from staff cards. The host collects the visitor at reception, stays with them throughout the visit, and returns them to reception to sign out and surrender the badge. Visitor badges open no doors. A visitor found unescorted is walked back to reception by any staff member, politely and without exception, and the host is reminded of this policy; a second occurrence for the same host goes to the host's manager.

Group visits, such as auditors or training providers, may be hosted by one person for up to five visitors. Larger groups need one host per five visitors. Visitors' access to the operations floor and the server and comms room follows section 3.

Deliveries are received at reception or the goods entrance and are not escorted into work areas by delivery personnel.

## 5. Lost and forgotten cards

A lost or stolen card is reported to facilities immediately on discovery, and outside office hours to the IT helpdesk, which can deactivate cards. The card is deactivated on report, before any investigation of whether it is merely mislaid. A replacement is issued after the holder's identity is confirmed against the HR System photo. Repeated losses are reported to the holder's manager.

A person who forgets their card obtains a day card from reception after photo verification against the HR System. Day cards carry the person's normal zones minus the server and comms room, expire at building close, and are surrendered at sign-out. Being let in by colleagues all day instead of getting a day card defeats the entry log and is not acceptable.

## 6. Tailgating

Tailgating is passing through a controlled door on someone else's card presentation, in either direction of intent: slipping in behind a colleague, or holding the door open as a courtesy. Both are prohibited at every controlled door. Each person presents their own card even when entering together, so the entry log stays complete. Challenging politely is expected behaviour: anyone may ask a person without a visible badge to present their card or accompany them to reception, whatever the person's apparent seniority. Staff are not disciplined for challenging in good faith, including challenging executives.

## 7. Leavers and card return

Leavers surrender their card to facilities on their last day as part of the leaver steps in the Joiner, Mover, Leaver Procedure. Facilities deactivates the card at end of the leaving day, or within one hour of notification for immediate dismissals, matching the revocation timelines for system access. An unreturned card is deactivated on the same schedule and recorded in the card register.

## 8. Monitoring and review

Door controllers log every card presentation. CCTV covers entrances, the operations floor perimeter, and the server and comms room door; recordings are retained for 90 days and handled as pii under the Data Classification Standard (CU-POL-010). Facilities reviews out-of-hours entry weekly and the full card register quarterly, alongside the access reviews in the Access Review Procedure (CU-POL-005). Discrepancies between the card register and the HR System, such as an active card for a leaver, are treated as control failures and reported to the information security office.
