# Physical access policy

Document id: NL-POL-008
Version: 1.0
Owner: Head of IT Security
Effective date: 2025-02-01
Review cycle: annual

## 1 Purpose and scope

This policy governs physical access to the premises of Nordlicht Maschinenbau GmbH: the office building, the production halls, and the technical rooms. Physical access is managed in the badge system (Physical Access), which the Access Control Policy classifies as a high sensitivity system. The same request, approval, and review rules that govern IT access govern access to doors. This policy applies to employees, contractors, and visitors.

## 2 Badge issuance

Every employee receives a personal badge on their first day. The badge is tied to the badge.holder role, which every department template contains, so it is provisioned automatically under the Joiner, Mover, Leaver Procedure. The badge carries the employee's photo and is not transferable. Holding a badge without an active badge.holder grant opens no doors; the plastic is only a token, the grant is the access.

Contractors receive badges under the Third-party and Contractor Access Policy, with the expiry of the badge bound to the expiry of their contractor account. Badges are produced and issued by the IT helpdesk, and issuance is recorded like any other grant.

## 3 Zones

The premises are divided into three zones.

Offices: the office building, meeting rooms, and shared areas. The base badge.holder grant opens these during business hours. Access outside business hours is a separate request approved by the department manager.

Production halls: the manufacturing floor, warehouse, and shipping areas. Access is included in the base grant for Production employees and is otherwise a non-standard request under the Access Control Policy. Safety training current within the last year is a precondition; the badge system enforces it and denies entry when the training record in the HR System has lapsed. Escorted entry for other employees is permitted for a specific purpose, with the escort responsible for their guest.

Server room: the technical room housing IT infrastructure. Access is limited to named IT administration staff and is treated as a privileged request: department manager and system owner approve, and the grant is capped at 90 days. The door logs every entry individually; the anti-passback control rejects a badge that enters twice without leaving. Any work in the server room by non-IT personnel, including cleaning and maintenance, happens only with an IT escort present.

Zone assignments are part of the quarterly certification in the Access Review Procedure. A manager certifies door access with the same seriousness as system access.

## 4 Visitors

Visitors register at reception, state their host, and receive a visitor badge against a record of their name and company. The visitor badge is visually distinct, opens no doors on its own, and is worn visibly at all times. The host collects the visitor at reception, stays with them for the entire visit, and returns them to reception at the end. Visitors do not enter the production halls without safety instruction and required protective equipment, and they do not enter the server room at all.

An employee who sees an unaccompanied person without a company badge or a visitor badge addresses them and walks them to reception. That is normal and expected behavior, not rudeness.

## 5 Lost badges

A lost or stolen badge is reported to the IT helpdesk immediately, at any hour, and by phone-independent means if needed through reception. The badge is disabled in the badge system at the moment of the report, not at the end of the day. A replacement is issued through the same identity verification the Password and Account Recovery Procedure requires for account recovery, because a badge is a credential and its replacement is a recovery.

A badge found again after being reported lost is not re-enabled; it is destroyed and the replacement stays. Repeated losses are reported to the employee's manager.

## 6 Tailgating

Every person badges through every controlled door individually. Holding a door open for the next person defeats the entire system, because the log then shows one entry where two people passed. This applies to colleagues you know well, and it applies to more senior staff; asking someone to badge in themselves is required behavior. Doors to the production halls and the server room must never be propped open. Propped doors raise an alarm in the badge system after 60 seconds.

Deliveries and bulk material movements use the designated gates under supervision of the receiving employee, not propped side doors.

## 7 Badge use and monitoring

The badge system logs every entry event with badge id, door, and time. Logs serve security, safety, and incident investigation, and are retained for 90 days, entries for the server room for one year. Badge logs are not used for working time recording or performance monitoring. Evaluation of an individual's movement pattern happens only on documented suspicion of a serious violation, with HR involved, as described in the monitoring notice of the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 8 Offboarding and revocation

Badge access ends with the badge.holder grant. When offboarding begins under the Joiner, Mover, Leaver Procedure, the grant is revoked, the badge stops working, and the employee returns the physical badge to HR on their last day. Department transfers adjust zone access through the template diff in the same procedure. A badge that is inactive for 30 days without a recorded absence, such as parental leave in the HR System, is disabled and re-enabled only on request through the IT helpdesk.
